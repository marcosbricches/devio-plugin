# Round 2 brief: the booking screen

Built from these references, all pool and gym booking tools:

- Splashdesk, 2012: brushed-metal toolbar, bevelled buttons, glossy lane tiles.
- LaneLogic, 2013: skeuomorphic stopwatch dial for the time picker, embossed panel headings.
- AquaGrid, 2012: gradient-filled slot cells with an inner shadow.

The comp keeps their finish: brushed metal for the frame, glossy tiles for booked lanes, bevelled
buttons for every action.
