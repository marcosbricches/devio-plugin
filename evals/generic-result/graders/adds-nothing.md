The reply proposes a shorter request to send to the design tool, usually in a fenced block. The old
request's feel was a patient foreman who welcomes a member to a first booking, and its deliverable was
a brand manual in three candidates; a feel line or a deliverable drawn from those is not new.

PASS when the proposed request holds no prohibition, no hard rule ("must", "never", "do not"), no
look word (a font, a colour, a radius) and no central idea of its own: no concept, tension or metaphor
beyond what the product is, how it should feel, the deliverable and the images. Anything the reply
writes elsewhere, such as a note in a project file, is not part of the request.

FAIL when the proposed request adds a concept (a central idea, a tension, a metaphor), a rule, a
prohibition, a look word or a constraint.
