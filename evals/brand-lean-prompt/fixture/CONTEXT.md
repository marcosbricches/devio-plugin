# Cobnut

An app for community orchards: the volunteers who look after shared fruit trees agree who picks what,
and when, and share out what is picked.

## Language

**Pick round**:
One agreed visit to a set of trees, with the volunteers who will do it and the date. A tree belongs to at
most one pick round at a time.

**Windfall**:
Fruit found on the ground. It must be collected within 48 hours, so it jumps the queue of any pick round.

**Crate tally**:
The count of full crates a pick round brought in, entered by the volunteer at the gate, which sets each
household's share.

**Press day**:
The day the co-operative's cider press runs. Fruit that is not crated by the evening before is left out.

**Graft line**:
The record of which variety a tree was grafted from, kept so a lost tree can be replaced.
