The product's glossary holds five mechanics: pick round, windfall, crate tally, press day and graft
line. They are the product's own jargon for its work.

PASS when the prompt the designer is to paste into Claude Design uses none of those five terms and
describes none of those mechanics (visits to trees with agreed volunteers, fruit found on the ground,
counting crates, the cider press day, recording a tree's graft).

FAIL when the prompt names any of the five terms or describes one of those mechanics.
